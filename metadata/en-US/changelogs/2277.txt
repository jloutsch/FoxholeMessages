What's new in 2.9.8:
- Improved: the app's internals were rebuilt on Kotlin coroutines and Flow, replacing the old RxJava libraries. Nothing should look different.
- Fixed: Unblock in Blocked messages now unblocks the selected conversation.
- Fixed: replying from a notification or Android Auto no longer reads the whole conversation on the app's main thread, which could slow the reply in long conversations.
